# Aeria Home Page Content

## Positioning

Aeria is a premium upgrade studio for balconies, windows, open spaces and semi-open spaces.

Aeria leads with the whole space first. Pigeon nets and invisible grills sit inside the starting layer: safety, comfort and daily-use essentials. The larger brand promise is to make open and semi-open parts of a home more usable, cleaner, safer and better finished.

## Core Message

Upgrade the open spaces you already own.

Aeria improves balconies, windows and semi-open areas with premium safety, comfort and enhancement systems. We begin with essentials like bird protection, invisible grills, mosquito mesh, rain sheets and drying racks. The next layer brings in greenery, lighting, furniture and sound.

## Voice

- Premium, calm and direct.
- Fewer words. More confidence.
- Say what Aeria does before listing products.
- Keep problem language controlled. Keep the brand away from pest-control language.
- Avoid forced either-or positioning.
- Avoid inflated lifestyle language.
- Avoid em dashes. Use short sentences or commas.

## Page Structure

1. Hero: brand positioning.
2. Spaces: what parts of the home Aeria upgrades.

## Content Density Rules

- Hero lede: maximum 2 lines on desktop.
- Section ledes: one sentence where possible.
- Service card copy: one sentence only.
- Service bullets: maximum 3 per card.
- Coming soon cards: one sentence only.
- Process steps: one short sentence each.
- Skip animation and website-experience explanations in the copy.
- Balance the word balcony with windows, open spaces and semi-open spaces.
