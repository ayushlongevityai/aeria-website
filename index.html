FAQs
Answers to Your Frequently Asked Questions

Can my balcony handle heavy planters, flooring or a pergola?

It depends on structure and how your slab or steel is rated. We review available drawings when we have them, coordinate a structural review if the scope adds meaningful load (large planters, hot tubs, cantilevered shade, fixed seating), and design for realistic weights and drainage. We would rather right-size your palette than overload the edge that holds everything up.

Do you handle strata / HOA approvals and weatherproofing?

Yes. We prepare concise packs for strata councils and HOAs when alterations touch the envelope, rails, or fixed services—often paired with simple line drawings and finish samples. Weatherproofing is part of every specification: outdoor-rated finishes, concealed drainage, UV-stable textiles, and hardware that will not rust through in two seasons.

What does a typical balcony project cost and how long does it take?

Most full transformations we scope land in staged bands—refresh & styling, partial rebuild, or full terrace fit-out—after a fixed audit on site. Calendar time is usually four to eight weeks from locked drawings on typical access, faster for styling-only scopes. You receive a clear payment schedule tied to milestones, not guesswork.

Do you work on tiny balconies (under 4 m²)?

That is our sweet spot. Micro-balconies benefit from custom depth furniture, wall-hung planters, vertical storage, and lighting layers that make the space feel twice as usable. We treat the square centimetres with the same rigour as a full terrace—often more.
